Typography and Text Styling

Typography plays an important role in making a webpage attractive, readable, and easy to understand.

Importance of Good Typography

Good typography improves the readability of content and creates a better experience for users. Proper font selection and spacing can make information easier to understand.

Different font families, sizes, and weights can be used to create a clear visual hierarchy on a webpage. Headings should stand out, while normal text should remain comfortable to read.

Readable Text

A good combination of line-height, letter-spacing, and text alignment makes a paragraph more visually balanced. Typography should always focus on clarity and simplicity.
